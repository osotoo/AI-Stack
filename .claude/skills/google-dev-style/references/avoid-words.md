# Words to avoid or not use

The 231 word-list entries whose guidance is "don't use" or "avoid". Scan this
when you want to check a single word quickly. For the full 597-entry list,
including entries that only say how to spell or capitalize a term, see
`word-list.md`.

Two strengths of guidance:

- **Avoid / use with caution** — the term is ambiguous or obscure. Prefer the
  suggested alternative, but you can use the term if it genuinely fits.
- **Don't use** — the term is ambiguous enough to mislead, or carries an
  offensive or non-inclusive association. Replace it or write around it.
